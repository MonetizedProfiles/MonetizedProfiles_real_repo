### BRAND VOICE RULES (from codebase analysis)

Every email/SMS must follow these rules:
- **Tone:** Confident, casual, peer-to-peer. Write like a successful friend, not a corporation.
- **Trust signal in every email:** At least ONE of: 100% organic followers, 30-day replacement guarantee, 27,000+ sold, 4.8 rating, 1,277 reviews, $847K+ customer revenue
- **Urgency in every email:** Limited stock, restock unpredictability ("accounts must be organically grown"), time-limited offers
- **CTAs:** Action verb + outcome. "Start Earning Today", "Browse Available Accounts", "Get Started Risk-Free". NEVER "Click Here" or generic "Shop Now"
- **Key phrases to use:** "monetized accounts", "organic followers", "tax-approved", "full account ownership", "replacement guarantee", "earn on your first post", "skip months of grinding"
- **Avoid:** Corporate jargon, "Dear Customer", long paragraphs, cryptocurrency references

## SMS FLOWS

**Key rules:**
- Max 155 characters per message (leave room for dynamic content)
- Only send between 8 AM - 9 PM recipient local time
- STOP/HELP handling mandatory
- Max 2-4 promotional SMS per month
- SMS + email together = 35-55% cart recovery vs 10-12% email alone

### SMS Flow 1: Welcome (EXISTING - fix brand references)
- **Timing:** Immediate on SMS opt-in
- **Fix:** Change all "TikAccounts" → "MonetizedProfiles" and tikaccounts.com → monetizedprofiles.com

### SMS Flow 2: Abandoned Cart (NEW)

- **Limit:** 1 SMS per recipient per flow entry
- **Message:** "{{ first_name|default:'Hey' }}, your monetized account is still in your cart! Stock is limited - grab it before it sells: {{ event.extra.checkout_url }}"

### SMS Flow 3: Back in Stock Alert (NEW - highest ROI SMS flow)
